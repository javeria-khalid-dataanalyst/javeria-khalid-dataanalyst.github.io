About Me

Data is more than numbers—it's the story of a business waiting to be told. As a dedicated Data Analyst, I focus on building intuitive, actionable, and visually compelling data solutions.

My workflow goes beyond just creating charts. I handle end-to-end data transformation: cleaning raw data with Power Query (M), querying databases with MySQL, structuring efficient Data Models, and creating advanced DAX (YTD/YoY) measures for strategic business insights.
